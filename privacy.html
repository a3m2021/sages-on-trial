Privacy policy

Sages on Trial app · last updated 18 November 2026

The Sages on Trial app is a private tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer to publish videos to, and read statistics from, their own YouTube channel. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Google user data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl: to upload videos to the owner's channel and to set their title, description, privacy status and release time;
  • youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly: to read the owner's own channel and video statistics (views, watch time, retention).

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to measure how they perform. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and downloaded statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app, and they are not shared with any third party. Data goes only between the owner's computer and Google's YouTube APIs.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and statistics stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, which makes the stored tokens useless.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/a3m2021/sages-on-trial.
