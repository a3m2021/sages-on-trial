Sages on Trial

Ancient advice, cross-examined.

Sages on Trial is a YouTube Shorts channel. Each episode takes one well-known saying, such as "An apple a day keeps the doctor away", and checks it against published research: what holds up, what does not, and what is still unknown.

What this app does

The Sages on Trial app is a private production tool used only by the channel owner. It connects to the owner's own YouTube channel through Google sign-in in order to:

  • upload finished episodes to the owner's channel and schedule their release;
  • read view and retention statistics for the owner's own videos, to decide which topics to make next.

It does not access anyone else's account, and it is not offered to the public.
